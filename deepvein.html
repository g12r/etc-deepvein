Find the Mother Lode on Claim 7

Somewhere at the bottom of Claim 7 lies the Mother Lode, and McGraw's crew next door is digging for it too. Each day you get one lamp of oil: dig, haul your gold up, sell it when the price is right, and sleep. Read the quartz in the rock, pick your contracts and perks, crack geodes for crystals, and follow the old prospector's map.

    Mine rules

    One lamp a day

    Your lamp burns oil while you are underground: about 5 minutes. When it runs dry it is pitch dark. Sleep at the bunkhouse for a full lamp and a new day. Hang mine lanterns to light tunnels for good.

    Read the rock

    Ore is hidden. White quartz streaks mean gold is near, brighter means closer, and gold flecks mean it is right next door. A survey kit shows hidden ore in a rectangle.

    Sell smart

    The gold price changes every morning, and the telegraph tells you which way it goes tomorrow. Lock ore in the strongbox and sell on a good day.

    Contracts

    Three new offers come in each morning. You hold one at a time, and each has a deadline. Pick the one that fits what you plan to do.

    Perks

    Every promotion lets you choose 1 of 3 perks. So does every times table you master. No two miners end up the same.

    Geodes

    Round grey lumps in the rock. Crack them at the assay office: each is a times-table fact, and a streak of right answers pays more. You choose which table to work on.

    Old caches

    Three old prospector's caches are buried on the claim. Old rubble deep underground points to them. Each holds a piece of the map to the Mother Lode.

    More than gold

    Copper near the top, silver below it, gems in deep rock and rubies in granite. Fossils and old relics go in your museum collection.

    Pip the pack rat

    Out of ladders or timber down a shaft? Press R and Pip runs some down from the store. It costs double, and what you cannot pay goes on your tab until your next sale.

    Math pays

    Work out the weigh-in at the Assay Office and you get 50% more for the whole sale, or 75% more in under 3 seconds. Counting the cost at the store gets you free extras.

    Deeper is richer

    Water seeps in below 90 ft, so you need pumps. Deep rock (125 ft) needs a steel pick and pays 2 nuggets a block. Granite (225 ft) needs a diamond pick and pays 3.

    Roofs need timber

    A roof holds about 2 blocks from a wall in dirt, 3 in stone, 4 in deep rock. Past that it creaks and caves in. A timber post holds it up.

    Machines

    A hoist lifts ore to your strongbox. Belts and carts feed it. The elevator rides a whole shaft, and the steam drill bores tunnels by itself.
