    Mine rules

    Gold is hidden

    Ore looks like plain rock until you uncover it. Gold flecks mean gold is right next door. A survey kit shows hidden ore in a rectangle.

    Deeper is richer

    Deep rock (155 ft) needs a steel pick and its gold gives 2 nuggets a block. Granite (220 ft) needs a diamond pick and gives 3, plus gems. Dynamite breaks anything but bedrock.

    Water table

    Below about 135 ft, water seeps into your tunnels. You can't swing a pick underwater. Pumps drain everything around them.

    Carts or belts?

    Belts are automatic but slow. An ore cart on rails carries 24 and goes fast, but you drive it. Park it next to a hoist bucket to unload.

    Roofs need timber

    A roof holds about 2 blocks from a wall in dirt, 3 in stone and 4 in deep rock. Past that it creaks, cracks and caves in. A timber post holds it up.

    Dig, then fill

    Every block you dig gives you fill rock, up to 40. Use it to build stair-steps, plug holes or put a roof back.

    Gold goes out

    The sack holds 6 and slows your climbing. A hoist over any shaft sends ore up to the assay office. Belts can feed ore straight into its bucket.

    Machines

    Conveyors run the way you face when you place them. Click one with the belt tool to flip it. The elevator rides a whole shaft. The steam drill bores a tunnel on its own and drops ore behind it.

    Where the math lives

    Assay office: work out the pay for a 25% bonus. Store: count a bundle for a free extra. Dynamite: count the charges. Survey: work out the area.
